Every component receives an implicit, protected prop called **`children`** — it holds whatever markup the parent wrote between the component's opening and closing tags. Render it with `{$render children}`.

### Your Task

1. Open `components/panel.wire`.
2. Replace the comment with `{$render children}`.
3. The `<p>` from `index.wire` should now appear inside the panel's styled `<section>`.

`children` is a **`Snippet`** value, not a string. The framework only runs it when you render it, so wrapping children in `{$if}` or `{$for}` is free — conditionals and loops don't force the body to evaluate until needed.
